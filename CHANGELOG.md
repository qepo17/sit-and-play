# Changelog

What changed in each version of Sit and Play, newest first.
The app shows a version's notes when it updates to that version.
Write them as plain lines and `- ` list items, for players rather than developers.

## Unreleased

### Added

- Sit and Play updates itself. New versions download in the background and start the next time you open the app.
- Settings → General shows the version you have and lets you check for updates. Restart from there to use a downloaded update right away.

## [0.0.2] - 2026-10-09

### Changed

- The home screen shows only installed games. Find the others in your library.

## [0.0.1] - 2026-10-09

The first release.

### Added

- Browse the games installed and played on this computer, with their artwork, descriptions, prices, news, and playtime.
- Play or install games through the Steam client.
- Browse the Steam store in a window of the app.
- Use a keyboard, mouse, or controller, with Xbox, PlayStation, and Nintendo button hints.
- Change the keyboard shortcuts and controller buttons, and start in full screen.
- Connect a Steam account to add the games you own but never installed, and see your achievements.
